# Offline Operation

Turn on **Options > Offline Operation** when you want TV Rename to work only with the show and movie information it has already downloaded, for example when you have no internet connection.

When you choose it, TV Rename asks "Are you sure you wish to go offline?". Click `Yes` to go offline, or `No` to stay online. The menu item is ticked while you are offline. Choose it again to go back online. You are not asked when going back online.

While you are offline:

* TV Rename doesn't download show and movie information when you scan or add a show. It uses what it already has.
* `OFFLINE` shows on the right-hand side of the [status bar](../../main/other.md#the-status-bar).
* The **TV Shows** tab shows a simpler summary of the selected show or season instead of the full details.
* [Tools > Background Download Now](../tools-menu/background-download-now.md) asks "Ignore offline mode and download anyway?" before downloading.

To stop the regular background updates as well, untick [Options > Automatic Background Download](automatic-background-download.md).

TV Rename can also go offline by itself: if it can't connect to a data source, the **Cannot Connect** window that appears has a `Go Offline` button.
