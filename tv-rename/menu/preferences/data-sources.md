# Data Sources

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/prefs-03-data-sources (1).png>)

These settings control information sources and refreshes.

* **Default TV Show Source** – choose The TVDB, TV Maze or TMDB. A show can override it in **Source** on [Add/Edit Show](../../main/my-tv-shows.md#the-basics-tab).
* **Default Movie Source** – choose The TVDB or TMDB. A movie can override it in [Add/Edit Movie](../../main/movies.md#basics).
* **Download up to** – set simultaneous show and image downloads from 1 to 8.
* **Update cache every** – refresh information every 1 to 96 hours while [Automatic Background Download](../options-menu/automatic-background-download.md) is enabled.
* **Release Date Type** – **Regional** uses the custom or preferred region. **Global** uses the earliest date worldwide.

### TheTVDB

* **Preferred language** – language requested from TheTVDB.
* **Refresh entire series if** – re-download a show when this percentage of episodes changes.
* **Version** – TheTVDB service version (**v4**).

### TV Maze

TV Maze has no settings.

### TMDB

* **Preferred language** – language requested from TMDB.
* **Preferred region** – region used for release dates and certifications.

_Defaults: Default TV Show Source and Default Movie Source **TMDB**, **4** downloads at a time, update every **1** hour, **Regional**, **20%**._
