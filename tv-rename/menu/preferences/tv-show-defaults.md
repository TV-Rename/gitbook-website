# TV Show Defaults

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/prefs-06-tv-show-defaults (1).png>)

These settings are starting values for new shows. They do not change existing shows. Edit them on [TV Shows](../../main/my-tv-shows.md), or use [Options>Settings Check](../options-menu/settings-check.md).

### Default TV Show Settings

* **Use Automatic Season Folders** – enables automatic season folders for new shows.
* **Use Default Location** – uses the selected TV Library Folder and its show-folder format. This also needs **Use Automatic Season Folders**.
* **Default season location** – choose **in base folder** or **in subdirectories**.
* **Airs in Timezone** – time zone for new shows. Without one, TV Rename uses America/New\_York.
* **Ignore Specials** – starts new shows with season 0 ignored.

### Default Advanced Settings

These are starting ticks for the [Advanced tab](../../main/my-tv-shows.md#the-advanced-tab). **Use DVD Order** and **Use Alternate Order** cannot both be ticked.

For missing episodes only:

* **Use sequential number matching** – match by the episode’s overall show number.
* **Look for airdate in filenames** – match by air date.
* **Look for episode title in filenames** – match by episode title.
