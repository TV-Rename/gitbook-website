# Background Download Now

**Tools > Background Download Now** (Ctrl+B) checks TheTVDB, TMDB and TVmaze straight away for changes to the shows and movies in your library, and downloads anything that has changed. It is the same update that [Automatic Background Download](../options-menu/automatic-background-download.md) runs on a timer, and it works whether or not that option is ticked.

While it runs, the menu item is greyed out and the right-hand side of the [status bar](../../main/other.md#the-status-bar) shows what is being downloaded. When it finishes, TV Rename saves the information it downloaded and updates the **Schedule** tab. If nothing has changed it finishes almost straight away.

If [Offline Operation](../options-menu/offline-operation.md) is on, TV Rename asks "Ignore offline mode and download anyway?". `Yes` downloads this once and you stay offline. `No` cancels.

If Automatic Background Download is ticked, its next update is counted from now.
